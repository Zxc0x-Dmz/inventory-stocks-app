Staff

Each person signs in with their own PIN. Mechanics can take, return, write off, receive, restock, seal and count. Supervisors can also accept count differences and change locations, parts and staff.

Part photos

Load the photos from your Spare Parts Catalogue Preview.html file once (on the laptop). They are made small and saved in the Photos tab of your Google Sheet, so every phone can show them. You can run it again any time.

Add / edit a bin or tote

Bins: R1-S3-B04 (or R1-S3-B04-A for a divided bin). Sealed totes: T-0001. Opening quantity is only used for a new location and is logged.

Load many bins at once

One per line: location, SKU, quantity, minimum. Use this to enter today's year-end count. Lines for locations that already exist are skipped.

New part

You can add a part that is not in the list yet. A supervisor gives it a bin, and changes existing parts.

SKU format MODEL-SYSTEM-PART, e.g. NB3-BDY-RFND. Systems: ELC electrical, WHL wheels & brakes, BDY body & frame, DRV drivetrain, FST fasteners.

The photo is found in Google Drive by NetSuite SKU (file name "1234567 - Part name.jpg").
